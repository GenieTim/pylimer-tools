Inactive Strand & Entanglement Removal in Force Balance 2¶

In polymer network modeling via the Maximum Entropy Homogenization Procedure (MEHP) and Force Balance (Gusev [Gus19], Bernhard and Gusev [BG25]), networks generated below or near full conversion contain significant fractions of dangling chains, sol fractions, and topological loops.

In MEHPForceBalance2, entanglements are modeled as static topological constraints (fixed 4-functional links or Hookean springs) without contour slipping. This introduces two interrelated and critical modeling questions:

  1. The Sampling Trap (Pre-Filter vs. Unfiltered Sampling): If entanglements are sampled without filtering (sample_entanglements() with filter_dangling_and_soluble=False), entanglements are placed indiscriminately along all chains, including dangling ends and unattached sol molecules. Because links cannot slip off open chain ends in Force Balance 2, an entanglement linking a dangling chain to the gel effectively pins the dangling arm in space, artificially transforming dead-end chains into load-bearing network strands! Near the percolation threshold (\(p \approx 0.70 - 0.85\)), this produces a massive, unphysical inflation of the predicted shear modulus (\(G\) overestimated by 300% to > 4000%).

  2. Iterative Inactive Removal & Graph Simplification: Can the force balance relaxation procedure itself detect and eliminate these unphysical constraints? Yes! MEHPForceBalance2 provides dedicated structural simplification modes (StructureSimplificationMode):

    • NO_SIMPLIFICATION: Retains all nodes, strands, and springs. Dangling ends relax their internal tension to zero, but any static entanglements pinning them remain active constraints.

    • INACTIVE_ONLY: Iteratively detects inactive strands where end-to-end partial spring extensions are below a designated cutoff (inactive_removal_cutoff) or have dangling connectivity (degree 1), purging their springs and associated entanglement springs from the matrix.

    • INACTIVE_THEN_X2F: First removes all inactive strands and entanglements, then systematically collapses 2-functional crosslinkers and intermediate nodes (unlinking bifunctional nodes). Two springs in series with lengths \(N_1, N_2\) are replaced by a single equivalent spring of length \(N_1 + N_2\). This compresses the sparse linear system to only the elastically active junctions (\(f \ge 3\)).

This example provides an in-depth statistical investigation using large polymer networks (\(N_{\text{strands}} = 2{,}000\), \(N_{\text{atoms}} \approx 100{,}000\), \(N_{\text{ent}} \approx 7{,}500\)) across conversion \(p \in [0.70, 0.95]\) with multi-seed ensembles and 95% confidence intervals:

  • Modulus Convergence: How both topological pre-filtering and solver-level inactive removal recover the identical, physically correct equilibrium phantom modulus \(G(p)\).

  • Topological Reduction: How inactive pruning and 2-functional unlinking reduce the network size by up to 80%+ near percolation.

  • Degrees of Freedom & Solver Complexity: The substantial memory and matrix dimension reductions achieved.

  • Practical Guidelines: When to pre-filter, when to simplify, and recommended configurations.

Multi-Seed Ensemble Simulation Procedure¶

We generate polymer networks across a conversion range from \(p = 0.70\) (near the gelation threshold) to \(p = 0.95\) (dense, well-cured network). For each conversion and Monte Carlo realization, we evaluate 5 distinct modeling regimes:

  1. Unfiltered + No Simplification (NO_SIMPLIFICATION): Naive baseline where dangling chains receive entanglements and are retained in the matrix.

  2. Unfiltered + Inactive Pruning (INACTIVE_ONLY): Entanglements sampled everywhere, but inactive strands and dead-end entanglements are pruned during relaxation.

  3. Unfiltered + Full Simplification (INACTIVE_THEN_X2F): Inactive pruning followed by 2-functional node unlinking.

  4. Filtered + No Simplification (NO_SIMPLIFICATION): Topological leaf peeling during sampling (no entanglements on dangling chains), unsimplified solver.

  5. Filtered + Full Simplification (INACTIVE_THEN_X2F): Clean sampling combined with full topological simplification.

Results are cached in a committed .npz file for instantaneous CI and documentation builds.

def _simulate_inactive_removal_single_repeat(
    r: int,
    seed: int,
    box_l: float,
    b: float,
    n_strands: int,
    strand_len: int,
    conversions: Sequence[float],
    n_ent: int,
    cutoff: float,
    r02: float,
    vol: float,
    gamma_conv: float,
    inactive_cutoff: float,
) -> Tuple[
    int,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
    np.ndarray,
]:
    """Execute a single Monte Carlo realization across conversions and simplification modes."""
    n_conv = len(conversions)

    # Modulus arrays [kPa]
    g_unf_nosimp = np.zeros(n_conv)
    g_unf_inact = np.zeros(n_conv)
    g_unf_x2f = np.zeros(n_conv)
    g_filt_nosimp = np.zeros(n_conv)
    g_filt_x2f = np.zeros(n_conv)

    # Spring count arrays
    sp_unf_nosimp = np.zeros(n_conv)
    sp_unf_inact = np.zeros(n_conv)
    sp_unf_x2f = np.zeros(n_conv)
    sp_filt_nosimp = np.zeros(n_conv)
    sp_filt_x2f = np.zeros(n_conv)

    # Link count arrays
    links_unf_nosimp = np.zeros(n_conv)
    links_unf_inact = np.zeros(n_conv)
    links_unf_x2f = np.zeros(n_conv)
    links_filt_nosimp = np.zeros(n_conv)
    links_filt_x2f = np.zeros(n_conv)

    # Iteration count arrays
    iter_unf_nosimp = np.zeros(n_conv)
    iter_unf_inact = np.zeros(n_conv)
    iter_unf_x2f = np.zeros(n_conv)
    iter_filt_nosimp = np.zeros(n_conv)
    iter_filt_x2f = np.zeros(n_conv)

    for i, conv in enumerate(conversions):
        gen = pt.MCUniverseGenerator(box_l, box_l, box_l)
        gen.set_seed(seed + i * 1000)
        gen.set_bead_distance(b)
        gen.set_mean_squared_bead_distance(b**2)
        gen.add_strands(n_strands, [strand_len] * n_strands)
        gen.add_crosslinkers(int(0.5 * n_strands), 4)
        gen.link_strands_to_conversion(conv)

        # Entanglement sampling: filtered vs. unfiltered
        es_filt = gen.sample_entanglements(
            n_ent, cutoff, 0.0, 0, 3.0, f"s_filt_{seed}_{conv}", True
        )
        es_unf = gen.sample_entanglements(
            n_ent, cutoff, 0.0, 0, 3.0, f"s_unf_{seed}_{conv}", False
        )

        # 1. Unfiltered + No Simplification
        fb1 = gen.get_force_balance2(es_unf)
        fb1.run_force_relaxation(
            simplification_mode=pt.StructureSimplificationMode.NO_SIMPLIFICATION
        )
        g_unf_nosimp[i] = (
            gamma_conv * np.sum(fb1.get_gamma_factors(r02)) / vol) * 1e6
        sp_unf_nosimp[i] = fb1.network.nr_of_springs
        links_unf_nosimp[i] = fb1.network.nr_of_links
        iter_unf_nosimp[i] = fb1.get_nr_of_iterations()

        # 2. Unfiltered + INACTIVE_ONLY
        fb2 = gen.get_force_balance2(es_unf)
        fb2.run_force_relaxation(
            simplification_mode=pt.StructureSimplificationMode.INACTIVE_ONLY,
            inactive_removal_cutoff=inactive_cutoff,
        )
        g_unf_inact[i] = (
            gamma_conv * np.sum(fb2.get_gamma_factors(r02)) / vol) * 1e6
        sp_unf_inact[i] = fb2.network.nr_of_springs
        links_unf_inact[i] = fb2.network.nr_of_links
        iter_unf_inact[i] = fb2.get_nr_of_iterations()

        # 3. Unfiltered + INACTIVE_THEN_X2F
        fb3 = gen.get_force_balance2(es_unf)
        fb3.run_force_relaxation(
            simplification_mode=pt.StructureSimplificationMode.INACTIVE_THEN_X2F,
            inactive_removal_cutoff=inactive_cutoff,
        )
        g_unf_x2f[i] = (
            gamma_conv * np.sum(fb3.get_gamma_factors(r02)) / vol) * 1e6
        sp_unf_x2f[i] = fb3.network.nr_of_springs
        links_unf_x2f[i] = fb3.network.nr_of_links
        iter_unf_x2f[i] = fb3.get_nr_of_iterations()

        # 4. Filtered + No Simplification
        fb4 = gen.get_force_balance2(es_filt)
        fb4.run_force_relaxation(
            simplification_mode=pt.StructureSimplificationMode.NO_SIMPLIFICATION
        )
        g_filt_nosimp[i] = (
            gamma_conv * np.sum(fb4.get_gamma_factors(r02)) / vol) * 1e6
        sp_filt_nosimp[i] = fb4.network.nr_of_springs
        links_filt_nosimp[i] = fb4.network.nr_of_links
        iter_filt_nosimp[i] = fb4.get_nr_of_iterations()

        # 5. Filtered + INACTIVE_THEN_X2F
        fb5 = gen.get_force_balance2(es_filt)
        fb5.run_force_relaxation(
            simplification_mode=pt.StructureSimplificationMode.INACTIVE_THEN_X2F,
            inactive_removal_cutoff=inactive_cutoff,
        )
        g_filt_x2f[i] = (
            gamma_conv * np.sum(fb5.get_gamma_factors(r02)) / vol) * 1e6
        sp_filt_x2f[i] = fb5.network.nr_of_springs
        links_filt_x2f[i] = fb5.network.nr_of_links
        iter_filt_x2f[i] = fb5.get_nr_of_iterations()

    return (
        r,
        g_unf_nosimp,
        g_unf_inact,
        g_unf_x2f,
        g_filt_nosimp,
        g_filt_x2f,
        sp_unf_nosimp,
        sp_unf_inact,
        sp_unf_x2f,
        sp_filt_nosimp,
        sp_filt_x2f,
        links_unf_nosimp,
        links_unf_inact,
        links_unf_x2f,
        links_filt_nosimp,
        links_filt_x2f,
        iter_unf_nosimp,
        iter_unf_inact,
        iter_unf_x2f,
        iter_filt_nosimp,
        iter_filt_x2f,
    )


def run_inactive_removal_and_sampling_simulation(
    conversions: Sequence[float] = (0.70, 0.75, 0.80, 0.85, 0.90, 0.95),
    n_strands: int = 2000,
    strand_len: int = 50,
    box_l: float = 50.0,
    n_repeats: int = 8,
    base_seed: int = 42,
    inactive_cutoff: float = 1e-4,
    max_workers: Optional[int] = None,
) -> Dict[str, Any]:
    """Execute multi-seed ensemble simulation comparing inactive strand removal and sampling."""
    params = get_parameters_for_polymer(
        "PDMS", parameter_type=ParameterType.GAUSSIAN)
    b = params.get("<b>").to(params.get("distance_units")).magnitude
    cutoff = params.get_sampling_cutoff()
    ent_density = params.get_entanglement_density()
    r02 = params.get("R02").to(params.get("distance_units") ** 2).magnitude
    gamma_conv = (
        (params.get("T") * params.get("kb") / (params.get("distance_units") ** 3))
        .to("MPa")
        .magnitude
    )

    vol = box_l**3
    n_ent = int(ent_density * vol)
    effective_workers = resolve_max_workers(max_workers)

    print(
        f"Simulating Inactive Removal & Sampling study: {n_repeats} repeats, "
        f"{len(conversions)} conversions, N_strands={n_strands}, N_ent={n_ent} across {effective_workers} workers..."
    )

    n_conv = len(conversions)
    all_g_unf_nosimp = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_g_unf_inact = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_g_unf_x2f = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_g_filt_nosimp = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_g_filt_x2f = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))

    all_sp_unf_nosimp = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_sp_unf_inact = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_sp_unf_x2f = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_sp_filt_nosimp = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_sp_filt_x2f = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))

    all_links_unf_nosimp = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_links_unf_inact = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_links_unf_x2f = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_links_filt_nosimp = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_links_filt_x2f = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))

    all_iter_unf_nosimp = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_iter_unf_inact = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_iter_unf_x2f = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_iter_filt_nosimp = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))
    all_iter_filt_x2f = np.zeros((n_conv, n_repeats))

    tasks = [
        (
            r,
            base_seed + r * 100,
            box_l,
            b,
            n_strands,
            strand_len,
            conversions,
            n_ent,
            cutoff,
            r02,
            vol,
            gamma_conv,
            inactive_cutoff,
        )
        for r in range(n_repeats)
    ]

    if effective_workers > 1:
        with ProcessPoolExecutor(max_workers=effective_workers) as executor:
            futures = [
                executor.submit(_simulate_inactive_removal_single_repeat, *t)
                for t in tasks
            ]
            completed = 0
            for fut in as_completed(futures):
                (
                    r,
                    g1,
                    g2,
                    g3,
                    g4,
                    g5,
                    sp1,
                    sp2,
                    sp3,
                    sp4,
                    sp5,
                    lk1,
                    lk2,
                    lk3,
                    lk4,
                    lk5,
                    it1,
                    it2,
                    it3,
                    it4,
                    it5,
                ) = fut.result()
                all_g_unf_nosimp[:, r] = g1
                all_g_unf_inact[:, r] = g2
                all_g_unf_x2f[:, r] = g3
                all_g_filt_nosimp[:, r] = g4
                all_g_filt_x2f[:, r] = g5

                all_sp_unf_nosimp[:, r] = sp1
                all_sp_unf_inact[:, r] = sp2
                all_sp_unf_x2f[:, r] = sp3
                all_sp_filt_nosimp[:, r] = sp4
                all_sp_filt_x2f[:, r] = sp5

                all_links_unf_nosimp[:, r] = lk1
                all_links_unf_inact[:, r] = lk2
                all_links_unf_x2f[:, r] = lk3
                all_links_filt_nosimp[:, r] = lk4
                all_links_filt_x2f[:, r] = lk5

                all_iter_unf_nosimp[:, r] = it1
                all_iter_unf_inact[:, r] = it2
                all_iter_unf_x2f[:, r] = it3
                all_iter_filt_nosimp[:, r] = it4
                all_iter_filt_x2f[:, r] = it5

                completed += 1
                print(
                    f"  [Inactive Removal] Completed repeat {completed}/{
                        n_repeats
                    } (repeat index {r + 1})..."
                )
    else:
        for t in tasks:
            (
                r,
                g1,
                g2,
                g3,
                g4,
                g5,
                sp1,
                sp2,
                sp3,
                sp4,
                sp5,
                lk1,
                lk2,
                lk3,
                lk4,
                lk5,
                it1,
                it2,
                it3,
                it4,
                it5,
            ) = _simulate_inactive_removal_single_repeat(*t)
            all_g_unf_nosimp[:, r] = g1
            all_g_unf_inact[:, r] = g2
            all_g_unf_x2f[:, r] = g3
            all_g_filt_nosimp[:, r] = g4
            all_g_filt_x2f[:, r] = g5

            all_sp_unf_nosimp[:, r] = sp1
            all_sp_unf_inact[:, r] = sp2
            all_sp_unf_x2f[:, r] = sp3
            all_sp_filt_nosimp[:, r] = sp4
            all_sp_filt_x2f[:, r] = sp5

            all_links_unf_nosimp[:, r] = lk1
            all_links_unf_inact[:, r] = lk2
            all_links_unf_x2f[:, r] = lk3
            all_links_filt_nosimp[:, r] = lk4
            all_links_filt_x2f[:, r] = lk5

            all_iter_unf_nosimp[:, r] = it1
            all_iter_unf_inact[:, r] = it2
            all_iter_unf_x2f[:, r] = it3
            all_iter_filt_nosimp[:, r] = it4
            all_iter_filt_x2f[:, r] = it5
            print(
                f"  [Inactive Removal] Completed repeat {
                    r + 1}/{n_repeats}...")

    # Compute ensemble statistics
    stat_g_unf_nosimp = compute_ensemble_statistics(all_g_unf_nosimp, axis=1)
    stat_g_unf_inact = compute_ensemble_statistics(all_g_unf_inact, axis=1)
    stat_g_unf_x2f = compute_ensemble_statistics(all_g_unf_x2f, axis=1)
    stat_g_filt_nosimp = compute_ensemble_statistics(all_g_filt_nosimp, axis=1)
    stat_g_filt_x2f = compute_ensemble_statistics(all_g_filt_x2f, axis=1)

    stat_sp_unf_nosimp = compute_ensemble_statistics(all_sp_unf_nosimp, axis=1)
    stat_sp_unf_inact = compute_ensemble_statistics(all_sp_unf_inact, axis=1)
    stat_sp_unf_x2f = compute_ensemble_statistics(all_sp_unf_x2f, axis=1)
    stat_sp_filt_nosimp = compute_ensemble_statistics(
        all_sp_filt_nosimp, axis=1)
    stat_sp_filt_x2f = compute_ensemble_statistics(all_sp_filt_x2f, axis=1)

    stat_links_unf_nosimp = compute_ensemble_statistics(
        all_links_unf_nosimp, axis=1)
    stat_links_unf_inact = compute_ensemble_statistics(
        all_links_unf_inact, axis=1)
    stat_links_unf_x2f = compute_ensemble_statistics(all_links_unf_x2f, axis=1)
    stat_links_filt_nosimp = compute_ensemble_statistics(
        all_links_filt_nosimp, axis=1)
    stat_links_filt_x2f = compute_ensemble_statistics(
        all_links_filt_x2f, axis=1)

    stat_iter_unf_nosimp = compute_ensemble_statistics(
        all_iter_unf_nosimp, axis=1)
    stat_iter_unf_inact = compute_ensemble_statistics(
        all_iter_unf_inact, axis=1)
    stat_iter_unf_x2f = compute_ensemble_statistics(all_iter_unf_x2f, axis=1)
    stat_iter_filt_nosimp = compute_ensemble_statistics(
        all_iter_filt_nosimp, axis=1)
    stat_iter_filt_x2f = compute_ensemble_statistics(all_iter_filt_x2f, axis=1)

    return {
        "conversions": np.array(conversions),
        "n_repeats": np.array(n_repeats),
        "n_strands": np.array(n_strands),
        "strand_len": np.array(strand_len),
        "box_l": np.array(box_l),
        "vol": np.array(vol),
        "n_ent": np.array(n_ent),

Figure 1: Shear Modulus & The Dangling Chain Entanglement Trap¶

The left panel displays the equilibrium shear modulus \(G\) across conversion. Notice how the naive baseline (Unfiltered Sampling + NO_SIMPLIFICATION) severely overestimates \(G\) below \(p \approx 0.90\) due to pinned dangling chains.

Once inactive strands are pruned or dangling chains are pre-filtered, all curves collapse cleanly onto the true physical phantom percolation curve.

The right panel shows the relative error (inflation factor):

\[\text{Error} = \frac{G_{\text{unsimplified}} - G_{\text{true}}}{G_{\text{true}}} \times 100\%\]

demonstrating how unphysical constraints inflate stiffness by hundreds to thousands of percent!

Physical Interpretation & Practical Recommendations¶

  1. Why Unfiltered Entanglement Sampling Fails in Force Balance 2: In classical slip-link simulations (MEHPForceBalance with slipping enabled), a slip-link placed on a dangling chain can dynamically slide off the free chain end during deformation. In contrast, MEHPForceBalance2 models entanglements as static junctions. Without topological filtering, an entanglement between a dangling end and the gel artificially anchors the dangling chain, preventing it from relaxing and converting zero-modulus sol/dangling material into load-bearing network strands. At \(p \le 0.85\), this artifact inflates \(G\) by hundreds to thousands of percent!

  2. Analytical Brownian-Bridge Leaf Peeling is the First Line of Defense: Passing filter_dangling_and_soluble=True (the default in sample_entanglements() and get_force_balance2()) uses graph-theoretic leaf peeling to eliminate dangling and sol strands before entanglement pairs are sampled. This completely prevents artificial dangling entanglements.

  3. The Solver Pruning Rescues Corrupted Networks: Even if entanglements were sampled on an unfiltered network, running MEHPForceBalance2 with simplification_mode=StructureSimplificationMode.INACTIVE_THEN_X2F automatically detects all zero-tension dangling arms and prunes them, recovering the identical physical modulus (\(G_{\text{unfiltered+X2F}} \equiv G_{\text{filtered+X2F}}\)).

  4. Recommended Best Practice: - Always enable filter_dangling_and_soluble=True during entanglement sampling. - For subsequent large-scale deformation studies (e.g. uniaxial, biaxial, or shear strain sweeps),

    perform an initial relaxation with simplification_mode=StructureSimplificationMode.INACTIVE_THEN_X2F. This permanently purges inactive degrees of freedom and unlinks bifunctional nodes, accelerating every successive deformation step by 20–80%.
